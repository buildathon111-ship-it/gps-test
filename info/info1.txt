PROJECT: AGRIVISION — Environmental Intelligence & Agricultural Risk Engine

GOAL
Build a standalone Environmental Intelligence module for AGRIVISION.

The purpose is to take the geographic location of an agricultural field (latitude/longitude and eventually the complete field polygon) and collect relevant environmental, weather, climate, hydrological and satellite-derived information.

The system must transform raw environmental data into understandable agricultural risk indicators for:

1. Drought
2. Heat waves / heat stress
3. Flood risk
4. Water stress / irrigation requirement
5. General environmental/agricultural risk
6. Vegetation stress when satellite data is available

This module directly addresses the AGRIVISION competition problem statement, which explicitly requires early detection of droughts, floods, heat waves and other agricultural risks.
